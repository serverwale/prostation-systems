Unreal Engine 5 Workstation Checklist: Epic's Specs, Lumen, Nanite and Build Sizing

Epic Games publishes the hardware it recommends for Unreal Engine 5, and its own reference workstation is far larger than the baseline. This checklist turns the published figures into a sizing decision. The full guide is ProStation's best workstation for Unreal Engine 5 and game development.

What Epic recommends

ItemEpic's recommended hardware
Operating systemWindows 11
ProcessorQuad-core Intel or AMD, 2.5 GHz or faster
Memory32 GB RAM
Graphics memory8 GB or more
Graphics cardDirectX 12 compatible, with the latest drivers

For compiling, Epic says a machine with at least 12 to 16 cores is a practical local baseline when you are not using a distributed build solution, and it recommends Unreal Build Accelerator (UBA) for distributed compilation.

Lumen and Nanite

Lumen global illumination, reflections and MegaLights need DirectX 12 and Shader Model 6 enabled in Project Settings, and one of these graphics cards: an AMD RX 6000 series or newer, an Intel Arc A-series or newer, or an NVIDIA RTX 2000 series or newer. Nanite needs Windows with DirectX 12 Agility SDK support. These are entry conditions; how fast a level runs depends on the level.

Epic's own reference workstation

Epic lists a reference system: an AMD Ryzen Threadripper PRO 7985WX, 256 GB of DDR5-4800 ECC RDIMM memory, a 2 TB system drive, a 4 TB data drive and an NVIDIA RTX 4080 with 16 GB. Two honest points. It shows the shape of a serious machine: many cores, lots of memory, a large separate data drive and a 16 GB GPU. And ProStation Systems builds use Intel Xeon processors and ECC DDR4, not AMD or DDR5, so we match the shape, not the exact parts.

FAQ

  • What are Epic's recommended specs for Unreal Engine 5? — Epic's page lists Windows 11, a quad-core Intel or AMD processor at 2.5 GHz or faster, 32 GB of RAM, 8 GB or more of graphics memory and a DirectX 12 compatible graphics card with the latest drivers.
  • Which graphics cards does Lumen support? — Epic lists an AMD RX 6000 series or newer, an Intel Arc A-series or newer, or an NVIDIA RTX 2000 series or newer card, with DirectX 12 and Shader Model 6 enabled in Project Settings.
  • How many cores do I need to compile Unreal Engine? — Epic says that without a distributed build solution, a machine with at least 12 to 16 cores is a practical local-compile baseline, and that more cores continue to help. It recommends Unreal Build Accelerator for distributed compilation.
