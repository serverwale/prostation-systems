Workstation Checklist for Architects: Lumion, Twinmotion, D5 and SketchUp

Architectural visualization tools are GPU-led, and each vendor publishes what its software needs. This short checklist turns those published numbers into a build decision. The full reasoning, with the complete requirements table, is in ProStation's guide to the best PC for architects: Lumion, Twinmotion, D5 and SketchUp. Checklist before you order

  • Name the tools and versions you use every day, and the largest scene you have rendered.
  • Meet the vendor's recommended column for each tool, not the minimum.
  • Compare the single-thread CPU benchmark of the quoted processor with the vendor's figure.
  • Put the system on one NVMe drive and active projects on another, with a separate backup.
  • Plan cooling and noise for long renders; read the thermal throttling guide and the quiet workstation guide.
  • Size RAM with the RAM guide by profession.
  • Ask for the exact GPU, RAM, storage, warranty and delivery time in writing.

FAQ

  • How much video memory do I need for Lumion? — Lumion's requirements page lists 6 GB or more for its minimum tier, 10 GB or more for the recommended tier and 16 GB or more for the high-end tier. It also says 4K rasterization and 1080p ray tracing need at least 8 GB.
  • Do I need ECC memory for architectural rendering? — No. None of the Lumion, Twinmotion, D5 Render or SketchUp requirement pages lists ECC as a requirement. ProStation Systems builds use ECC DDR4 by default, which adds stability on long renders.
  • Is 32 GB of RAM enough for D5 Render? — D5 lists 32 GB as its recommended memory and 128 GB as optimal. Twinmotion lists 16 GB at minimum and 64 GB for its high-end tier, and Lumion lists 32 GB recommended and 64 GB for high-end.
